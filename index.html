Biosignatures

Biosignatures are signs or evidence that could show that life exists, or once existed, somewhere beyond Earth. They can include certain gases, chemicals, or physical features that are difficult to produce without living organisms. Scientists use biosignatures when searching for life on other planets and moons.

Scientists search for biosignatures by studying rocks, soil, atmospheres, and other materials. Telescopes can examine the atmospheres of distant exoplanets to look for gases that could be produced by life. Spacecraft and rovers can also collect and study samples from places such as Mars. Scientists must be careful because some biosignatures can also be created by non-living processes.

One example of a possible biosignature is oxygen in a planet's atmosphere, because living organisms on Earth produce large amounts of it. Scientists are also interested in gases such as methane and combinations of different gases that could suggest life. However, no biosignature found so far has been confirmed as proof of life beyond Earth.
